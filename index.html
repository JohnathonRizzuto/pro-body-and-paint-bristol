What We Do

Body, Paint & Everything Between

One shop for the whole job — insurance collision work, custom color, and ground-up classic revivals.

Photo: Collision job

Collision Repair

Bumper to frame. We handle the insurance estimate, pull the dents, and put your car back exactly the way it rolled off the lot.

Custom Paint & Refinish

Single-stage, base-coat clear, candy, metallic — sprayed in a downdraft booth and color-sanded to a wet, even shine.

Photo: Classic restoration

Classic & Restoration

Muscle cars, hot rods, weekend cruisers. Rust repair, panel fab, trim and that show-day finish — like the shop Nova.

Dent, Rust & Panel Work

Door dings, rust-through, creased quarters. We grind it back to clean metal, rebuild the shape, and prep it right.

Computerized Color Match

Faded panel or full repaint, we scan and mix to your factory code so the blend disappears in the sunlight.

Buff, Polish & Protect

Cut-and-buff to kill swirl marks, plus ceramic and sealant options to lock that finish in for the long haul.
